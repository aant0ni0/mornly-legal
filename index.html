Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Mornly is designed to keep your personal journal private. Your journal entries stay on your device and are not uploaded to Mornly's servers.

Who is responsible for your data?

Mornly is developed and operated by Antoni Michalczak, Poland, under the developer name Mornly.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact mornly.app@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

Your mood selections, goals, gratitude notes, journal answers, reflections, streak information, preferences and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. Mornly does not upload the contents of your journal to Firebase, RevenueCat or another Mornly-controlled server.

If you export your journal, the PDF is created only after you request it and is shared through Android's system share sheet. You decide where it is sent or saved.

Data processed by service providers

Mornly uses a limited number of service providers to operate and improve the app:

  • Google Firebase Analytics processes app interactions such as screens viewed, meditation activity, reminder settings and feature usage. Journal text is not included in analytics events.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, diagnostics, app state and device information to identify and fix technical problems.
  • Google Firebase Firestore delivers daily content to the app. Mornly does not send journal entries to Firestore.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing process purchase history, subscription status, product information and an app-specific customer or installation identifier so purchases can be completed and restored.

These providers may also process technical information such as app version, operating system, device model, locale, IP address or identifiers required to provide their services. Data transmitted to these providers is encrypted in transit.

How the data is used

Data processed outside your device is used only to provide app functionality, deliver daily content, process and restore purchases, understand general feature usage, maintain security, diagnose crashes and improve reliability. Mornly does not sell personal data or use journal contents for advertising.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Mornly schedules morning and evening reminders on your device. You can disable them in the app settings or Android system settings.

Retention and deletion

Local journal data remains on your device until you reset the app's data or uninstall the app. Analytics, diagnostics and purchase records are retained only as needed for the purposes described above, according to the applicable provider settings, legal requirements and provider retention policies.

Mornly does not provide user accounts. You can request deletion of data associated with Mornly's third-party service identifiers by contacting mornly.app@gmail.com. Some purchase records may need to be retained where required by law or by Google Play.

Children's privacy

Mornly is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.
